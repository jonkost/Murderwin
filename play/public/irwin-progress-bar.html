Notes for Claude Code
  • One file, no outside code. The art is traced from Jon's picture. The full brief is docs/handoff-2026-11-09.md.
  • ?embed=1 shows only the 16:9 screen, filling the window. Add &clear=1 for a clear background.
  • IrwinBar.start({seconds, elapsed, cards, onDone}) runs the whole screen. cards is a list of {tag, title, detail}.
  • From a parent page: post {type:'irwin:start', seconds, elapsed, cards} to the frame. It posts {type:'irwin:done'} back when the bar is full.
  • IrwinBar.setProgress(0 to 1) drives the bar by hand instead.
  • The frame posts {type:'irwin:ready'} to its parent once it is drawn, and again whenever it is sent {type:'irwin:hello'} (added 14 Nov 2026 with the wiring).
  • Near the top of the script: S is Irwin's size, BOW is when he bows.
