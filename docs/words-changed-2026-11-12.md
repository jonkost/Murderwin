# The words that changed on 12 November 2026

Jon, 12 Nov: "Anywhere there is random text that is not mission critical lose
it. The game needs to allow things to be focused in." Every screen a guest
sees is now a title, one short line at most, and one button. This page lists
every line that changed, old and new, so you can put any of them back with a
word. None of these is a clue, a role card, a news line or one of your rule
slides: those were not touched.

What the screens lost that is still worth saying to the room is in
`docs/read-aloud.md`, in the night's order.

## Left long on purpose

- Granolia's "Again, slower. I have all day. Well. Fifteen minutes."
- Boo's line to a trio's third player: your own words.
- Sir Irwin's prompts on the Parlour TV, his slides, his countdown and his
  trace lines.
- The arcade's how-to lines and the drawing's rules: they are a game's steps.
- The birthday card's "One candle. One very good boy."
- Each game's hook in the list of game names: your words. No guest card shows
  them now, but they are kept.
