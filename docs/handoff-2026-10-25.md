## 4 · Checks and wording

Six small things found while reading, none a decision.

- **Can the app make the phone buzz?** It already does where it can: Android
  phones buzz at the power's return and when a phone calls its guest. iPhones
  cannot buzz from a web page (Apple allows no app in a browser to do it), so
  on an iPhone the phone plays its own short chime instead, once sound has
  been allowed at sign-in. Nothing more to build; nothing in the plan should
  depend on a buzz.
- **The last line of the notes is cut off**: "Unless otherwise cued to do
  something every active screen should have a". Read as "a looping graphic",
  which is what cues 1, 3, 7, 12, 15, 19 and 22 say. Jon corrects it if that
  is wrong.
- **Names.** The field reporter is Mic Cameron (the cue list says Mike); the
  Study's cat is Granolia (the loop says Granola); the living room is the
  Parlour. The repo copy of the cue list uses the canon spellings.
- **Bulletin 1's voices.** Cue 6 credits bulletin 1 to Mic Cameron. As cast
  on 24 October, Susan opens and signs off bulletin 1 as Ava Waves and Jon
  reads the field parts as Mic Cameron. No conflict; the recording script has
  every line.
- **The ballot never closes early** (a client rule): the two minutes always
  run, even when every pick is in. Cue 20's "once all submissions are in" is
  Jon pressing Next after the two minutes, and Pick for the rest if someone
  is away.
- **The projector's warm-up.** Jon knows the projector takes a moment after
  its plug comes back on at the power's return. The Entryway code it shows in
  Act 2 is also printed, so nothing waits on it.
