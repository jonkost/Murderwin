### All five games

- **An exit button** in every game, to go back.
- **A game left unfinished doesn't count** toward a token (see arcadeExit in section 3).
- **Spell it "practice", with a C,** everywhere in the whole game, not only the arcade. The game says "practise", with an S, today.

### 1 · Tail

Fun, quick and a little challenging, and the controls are really good. The looks need work.

- **No more poo brown** on the tiles and dials. Use Boo's own colour, or something from Jon's branding.
- **Redraw the tail.** It still looks like poop.
- **The tail speeds up** as the game goes on.
- **Maybe the tail grows longer too,** to block Boo from reaching it.
- **Boo wraps around the edges,** Pac-Man style: off the top, in at the bottom; off the left, in at the right; and so on.
- **Bonus rewards:** other things to catch while chasing the tail.
- **Hazards:** things to avoid, which slow Boo down or make it harder.
- **Keep** the controls and the pace.
- **Check the start.** On Jon's first try the game was slow, so he went in again. The second try was fine.

### 2 · Laser Pointer

Jon: "This one's good." It needs a little more challenge.

- **Boo is faster.**
- **More obstacles** as time goes on and the game speeds up.
- **More close calls** with Boo.
- **A missed star costs points.** Today the safe play is to keep the dot far from Boo; this keeps the player moving. If stars stay on screen until caught, give each one a time limit.

### 3 · Whack-a-Mouse

Jon's favourite: "the best of the games." Barely change it.

- **More mice up at once** toward the end.
- **Keep** everything else.

### 4 · Knock

Jon: "kind of lackluster." It is too easy and drags. It only got good near the end, when it sped up.

- **Much faster,** and at top speed much earlier.
- **Friends pop up** along the way, which the player must not knock off.
- **Use the whole screen.** Today you only watch one part of it.
- **A tighter hit window.** It forgives too easily.
- **Make misses obvious.** Jon couldn't tell what the dots at the bottom mean, or whether he had missed any.

### 5 · Yarn

The controls must change, and the basket must be able to reach every ball.

- **Slide to move.** Slide a finger along the touch strip to move the basket left and right. No tapping.
- **The basket wraps around:** off one side, in at the other. Today some balls are too far away to reach, however fast you tap.
- **5 misses ends the round.**
- **Friends fall too,** which the player must not catch.
