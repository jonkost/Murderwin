The approved look

Jon said yes to these nine screens on 12 November 2026: “yes this is great.” They were drawn by Claude chat on a canvas Claude Code cannot open, so they are kept here, exactly as drawn.

For Claude Code: each screen is plain HTML and CSS inside a <template> in this file. Read the source for the sizes, colors, type and movement, and build the real screens to match (handoff of 12 Nov). The pictures are the game's own, from play/public, cut to each picture's own edges. Open this file from the docs folder so the pictures load.

On every screen: the navy and red wallpaper, dimmed by about half behind words; Abril Fatface for the big words; brass double lines; parchment cards; one wax-red button at most; and only the words a player needs.

1 · Room splash: The Study

Words: the room's name, and the cat's name on the red bar. Nothing else. Picture: Granola 1. The name sits in a marquee frame with slow bulbs; a few twinkles; the cat floats; the wallpaper drifts.

2 · A Mac's second display

Words: the room's name. Picture: Granola 2, and the seal. Calm: the cat floats, nothing else moves.

3 · Act 4 game card: Get Off My Lawn!

Words: the game's name, its three steps, and Scan to start under the room code (the dashed box stands in for the real code). Picture: Salem 2. Marquee bulbs round the name.

4 · A big win on a room screen

Words: Solved! Picture: Granola 4, hopping. The seal stamps in, paw prints burst out, twinkles.

5 · The End

Words: The End, and Happy first birthday, Sir Irwin. Picture: Irwin 4. Marquee bulbs, and confetti falling across the whole screen, behind the words.

6 · Credits: the dedication

Words, exactly as Jon wrote them, capitals and comma included: For Sydney, The Best Big Brother A Pup Could Ask For. The break after the comma is the only break. Picture: Sydney's photo, play/public/household/sydney.jpg, in a round brass frame, set at 50% across and 70% down so his nose stays inside the circle. A soft glow; nothing else moves.

7 · Phone: Now

Words: where to go, and one button. Picture: the room's cat looking over the card (Salem 3), the seal, top left. The camera button is top right on every tab. The button pulses.

8 · Phone: The vote

Words: The Vote, three rows (The Murderer, The Motive, The Ghost) and Lock in. Pictures: the portrait of each pick, cut to the head. The time left is a bar. Lock in pulses. The names shown are samples.

9 · Phone: A small win

Words: You met Miss Boo LaRue, and Done. Pictures: Boo 4, and the four cat faces (met: full color with a tick; not met: gray). The seal stamps in; confetti and twinkles.
