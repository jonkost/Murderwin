# The Workshop — what it is and how it behaves

Jon's private writing desk for the game. Everything on it is safe for you to see.
(The secret content — what the cats really reveal, the documents, who did it — is
never shown here. See `blind-content-protocol.md` for how that works.)

It's a website: https://murderwin-at-sirwin-manor.web.app/workshop/ — sign in with
Google. It works on your phone, the iPad or the Mac. It works offline; it catches
up when you're back online.

## 8 October 2026 — the plain-language pass

Jon asked for less, in plainer words, dark, and more like a form. Now:
- **Home** has five sections in everyday names (The characters, The household,
  Words on the phones, Blanks and frames, The bags), one sentence each, a bar
  per list. The rarely needed lists, the task board and the decisions sit
  under "Everything else".
- **Game night** holds the two hosts' names and the square code to save for
  the Act 1 slide. The night is run from the hosts' phones, not from here.
- Lists with a fixed shape (bios, rules, phone lines, act banners, bag items…)
  show two fields instead of one long line with format rules. What is saved
  does not change shape.
- The field guide is folded away under "More about this list".

## 12 October 2026 — the "what is this for" pass

Jenna could not tell what a list wanted, and neither could Jon. Now:

- **Every list opens with a card that never hides.** Four things, in this
  order: *Part of the night* (which act and which moment the list feeds, for
  example "Acts 2 and 4, the moment a cat starts talking to you"), what the
  list is, where a guest meets it, and what to type in each box, with one
  finished example laid out in the shape of the boxes. A button puts the
  example into the boxes to start from. The long rules fold away underneath.
  Focus mode ("three more") keeps the card; it only adds the goal bar.
- **One box per thing.** A character is three boxes (name, what they do, who they are; no costume, since characters are dealt at the door), a rule is a heading and
  a body, a bag item is the item, a sense and what it notices, a news frame is
  the words before the gap and the words after it (the gap is drawn for you).
  Dropdowns say plain words (Locked out, Warming up, In). What is saved does
  not change shape: the boxes are joined back into the same one line.
- **The new line goes at the top**, right under the card, and the newest
  lines sit under it. Enter moves to the next box; from the last box, Enter
  keeps the line. Speak writes into the box you are in. A half-typed line
  comes back if the tab dies. Lists with tabs remember the tab, and each tab
  shows its own count ("Boo 3 of 8").
- **Push and pull questions** replaced the filler messages (the old list is
  kept under Everything else, retired). One set is three silly questions in a
  row, two choices each, points on each choice. A question that needs it can
  take a third or fourth choice ("Add another choice"). Whatever a guest
  picks, the next question is the same. The ways through add themselves up on
  the screen (all eight listed; with more choices, the count and the range),
  and "See it the way a guest will" shows the phone: one question at a time,
  a button per choice, no numbers. Acts 2, 4 and 6 deal these to every phone
  at once. Decided by Jon on 12 October: a Detective's points move how the
  cats feel about them; the Murderer's and the Ghost's points move how clear
  the clues come out. Which sets the two hidden roles get, and how their
  points map to that, is decided blind on the night, so the numbers Jon
  writes never tell him which way the night moved.
- **Bars that fill.** Every list card has a bar that only fills and reads
  *Full* with the seal when it is done. Nothing is dealt, nothing nags,
  nothing turns red except a failed save. (A rank ladder and Sir Irwin lines
  were tried and removed; see 13 October below.)
- **Jenna's two words.** Under the card on every list: *Reads clearly* or
  *Needs another look*, plus one line of note. A list she has cleared carries
  a small "✓ read" on Home. The For Jenna page now opens the lists she should
  read first, and ends with a map of what every list is for.
- **Paste a lot at once.** Under the card on every list: "Have a lot written
  already? Paste it here and it fills itself in." Paste from Notes, a chat
  or a spreadsheet, or add a .txt, .md or .csv file. The workshop reads it
  into lines and boxes ("Name · Job" with the bio under it, "LOCKED: the
  line", "Heading: body", "Item, smell, minty", question sets with their
  choices and points), shows what it read, names any line it could not read,
  and adds the lot with one tap. Nothing is added until you tap.
- **How it looks in the game.** Beside the card, a small sketch of the phone
  (or room screen) drawn from your boxes as you type, or from the example
  when the boxes are empty. It shows where the words land and what sits
  around them: a cat's greeting above the secret middle, a rule on the Rules
  tab, a blank inside a cat's sentence, a bulletin on the wireless.
- **Examples say so.** The card's example is badged "Example only. Not yours,
  not saved." Example text in an empty box starts with "e.g." and appears
  only in the new-line boxes; a saved line's empty box says "Not written
  yet" (the fourteen characters all seemed to wear an apron before this).
- **Plain progress, no game.** The rank ladder and Sir Irwin's speech bubble
  were tried for a day and removed on 13 October: Jon could not tell what
  "the household stands at Cook" was asking of him. Home now says, in plain
  words, how many lines are written across all the lists, which list needs
  him most, and has one button into it. The three cards under it say "N of
  M written. Needs K more." Every list page says "This list needs N. K to go."
  Rule for every instruction on screen: say what to write and where it goes;
  never a line that needs decoding.
- **Fewer boxes, after a pass for anything the game never reads.** A
  character is name, what they do, who they are (no costume: dealt at the
  door). A household card is one pick (who, of the five) and one box; the
  name and title fill themselves in. Act banners are two lines, banner and
  objective; the hosts' "beats" line went, nothing on the night read it. The
  blackout cards are five: three cards and two briefings; the shared "power
  is back" message is already one of the phone's own lines. The phone's own
  lines list exactly the fifteen screens the phone has. Sir Irwin needs one
  standing line, not three; he is always in, so his tab offers only In.
  Granolia's trail-offs are retired, kept, not shown: she misremembers one
  detail instead. A second, independent check then found more: the fourteen
  names and jobs are fixed in the game (it deals them and the cats judge the
  job), so a character is now a pick of who plus the persona; the act names
  are fixed too (The Plot Thickens, Paws for Thought), so each act is one
  sentence of where to be and what to do, under its name; the blackout is
  three cards of matched length, no private briefings, because what a role
  does is said aloud in the Act 1 welcome; house history and the old filler
  are retired and no longer sent to the phones; retired lists are kept but no
  longer listed on Home. Still on the board for Jon to close by hand: the
  stale task cards (cast guests, guest list, event cards) and the decisions
  canon has already answered (Irwin's affection, the event deck, who fires
  cues).
- Decisions are Jon's alone; the panel says so and shows the Confirm button
  only to his account. The sign-in page for a new account says what to do in
  plain words.

## The screens

**Home.** Three things:
- *Progress* — one bar per kind of content, with how many lines you have out of
  how many the game needs. Tap a bar to open that list.
- *Task board* — to do / doing / done. Tap a card to edit it; the arrows move it
  between columns. Add your own cards.
- *Decisions waiting on you* — a question with two or three answers. Pick one,
  confirm, and it disappears.

**Lists** — one per kind of content (Boo's closers, the room pool, echo frames…).
- The whole list is visible. Nothing is dealt to you at random.
- A fresh, empty line always sits at the bottom, and the cursor is already in it
  when the page opens. Type, press Enter, and the line is saved and a new empty
  one appears. Shift+Enter puts a line break inside a line.
- Click any line to edit it in place. The ▲▼ arrows reorder. ✕ removes a line,
  with an undo button.
- Lists with tabs (one per cat, or one per pool) remember which tab you're on.
- The *field guide* at the top of each list says what these lines are for, the
  format rules, and good and bad examples. Tap "Full field guide" to open it.
- While you type, a small ⚠ note under a line tells you if it breaks a format rule.
- "saved ✓" in the corner means saved. "saving…" means give it a second.

**Stationery tracker** — the seven document designs, each with a done tick, a notes
box and a place to attach reference images.

**Assets** — every picture and sound the night needs, on two tabs: Pictures and
Sounds. Each one has a name and a done tick.
- "+ Upload pictures" (or sounds) adds a new line for each file you pick, named
  after the file. Rename it any time.
- You can also type the name of something you still need, before the file exists.
  "+ Add the picture" puts the file on it later.
- Pictures show a small preview; sounds get a play button.
- "Replace file" swaps in a new version. The old one is kept, never thrown away.
- Uploading needs a connection. Names and ticks still save offline.

**Filler messages** (under Player screens) — what an ordinary player's phone shows
while the Murderer and the Ghost secretly pick an event. Each message ends with
"Button: …" so it takes as long as a real choice. Nothing in them is about the case.

## Promises the workshop makes (learned the hard way)

1. **You pick what to work on.** Nothing random, ever.
2. **Nothing is ever deleted.** Removing a line only hides it, and every change you
   make is kept in a history. (A screen to browse that history is still to build;
   it's on the task board.)
3. **If a save fails, you see a red banner.** Silence means saved.
4. **You can keep typing with no signal.** Edits queue up and send themselves
   when you're back online.
5. **The first time the workshop opened, it loaded the starter content once.** It
   never does that again, so nothing you've written can be overwritten by a seed.

## For Claude Code (technical appendix)

Data model (Firestore):

- `safe_content/{area}` — doc per area: `{ items: [{id, text, cat?, pool?, act?, done?, deleted, order}] }`
  Areas, grouped as on the dashboard (goals in parentheses; × means per tab):
  - Cat voices: openers (8×cat), closers (8×cat), refusals = the cat deflection
    lines (8×cat; the Firestore key predates the name), smalltalk (8×cat),
    trailoffs (8, Granolia only), status_lines (3×cat)
  - World & cast: npc_cards (5), bios (14), lore (4)
  - Player screens: rules_text (10), ui_copy (24), role_cards (6), event_cards (12),
    filler (20; innocent-screen beats for event windows, lint wants `Button:`)
  - Frames & pools: pools (10×pool), echo_frames (8), epilogue_frames (2), templates (7)
  - Show night: act_scripts (3×act), ops_docs (12)
  - Assets (checklists, items carry `done`): art_assets (Pictures), av_assets
    (Sounds). Edited on `/assets`; items may carry `file: {path, url, name, type, size}`.
    Uploads go to Storage at `assets/{area}/{itemId}/{ts}-{name}`; a replace writes
    a new path, so old files survive (storage.rules allow staff read/write, no delete
    guard yet).
  Areas, tasks and decisions added after the first seed import are created by
  additive migrations (`ADDITIONS` in `app/src/lib/content.js`) guarded by
  `meta/migrations` — additions only, existing data never rewritten.
- `safe_content/scenarios` — the push and pull questions. Items carry
  `beats: [{q, n, a, av, b, bv, c?, cv?, d?, dv?}] × 3` (`n` = 2 to 4 choices
  shown; arrays of maps, never nested arrays) and
  `text` mirroring the first question so the trail and the undo toast can name
  the set. `app/src/lib/scenarios.js` (pure, tested) normalises, totals the
  eight paths and lints. The play side should read this area, deal one set per
  phone per window, and never show a number.
- `meta/reviews` — `{ [area]: {state: 'clear'|'again'|'', note, by: {name}, at} }`,
  Jenna's two words per list. Written with merge, logged to history as area
  `reviews`.
- `countLinesBy` in `store.firestore.js` is kept but unused (the rank
  layer that used it was removed on 13 October).
- `app/src/lib/paste.js` (pure, tested) reads pasted text per list:
  `parsePaste(area, raw, { fields, codec })` and `parseScenarios(raw)`.
  Header lines ("Name · Job", "LABEL: …") open an entry; plain lines attach
  to the one above; blank lines separate; bullets are dropped. Added lines go
  through `applyChange` with one history row each. `PastePanel.jsx` is the
  UI; `GamePreview.jsx` holds the per-list sketches.
- One saved line, many boxes: `app/src/lib/codecs.js` (pure, tested with
  `npm --prefix app test`) holds the split/join for every multi-box list. The
  invariant: for a well-formed line, `join(split(x)) === x`; a line that does
  not fit lands whole in the main box and comes back unchanged.
- Preview mode for looking at screens without signing in (dev server only):
  `http://localhost:5173/workshop/?preview=1#/` swaps the store for an
  in-memory one (`app/src/lib/preview.js`). Never built into the live site.
- `workshop_history/{autoId}` — append-only: `{area, itemId, before, after, ts}`.
- `tasks/{autoId}` — `{title, detail, status, order, ts}`.
- `decisions/{key}` — `{question, options, answer, answeredAt}`.
- `sealed/*`, `cases/*` — Cloud Functions only. Client reads DENIED by rules.
- `validation_reports/{autoId}` — counts-only aggregates, client-readable; the
  dashboard's "Sealed coverage" panel renders the latest one.

Behaviour: autosave 400 ms debounce through a Firestore transaction that merges
pending changes onto the current remote items (never a whole-array overwrite) and
appends history in the same commit. Persistent local cache = the offline queue.
Rules deny `delete` on every workshop collection. Seed import once, guarded by
`meta/seeded`. Field guides come from `seed/safe-content.json` at build time,
not from Firestore. Auth: Google sign-in, UID allowlisted in `firestore.rules`.
