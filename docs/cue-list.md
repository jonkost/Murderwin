## What the night needs

**Scenes (music):** the default jazz; the Study light-game music; the
Orangerie game music; intense music at 35%; intense music at 60%; the
credit-roll fun music.

**Audio:** the chimes; a Wilhelm scream; the radio broadcast.

**Video:** the intro welcome video; the Act 2 news bulletin; the Act 7 news
bulletin reveal.

**Motion graphics and graphics:** the Study loop with Granolia; the Parlour
loop with Irwin; the JKIPP loop with Zimothy in the Library; the JKIPP loop
with Salem in the bedroom; the SKMBP projector loop with Irwin's portrait and
Boo walking, projection-mapped to the space above the Parlour; Irwin's
progress bar for tallying the submissions; the light-wall layouts of the game
rules; the floor-plan designs for the intro and the rules.

**What Jon needs Claude to make:** the credit roll; the tallying-submissions
roll; all the phone controls and cues; every light cue landing correctly;
every audio cue landing correctly; the QR slides; the rules for the cat games
and the drawing game. The splashes and slides use the full images of the cats
and the dog for their own content; an image may be used more than once, but
switch it up.

## Other notes

- A Notes area in the app.
- The blackout has to hit, and hit hard.
- The drawing game's timing: 15 seconds instead of 10. It is really fun, but tricky.
- Can the app make the phone buzz? (Answered in the 25 October handoff: Android phones buzz; iPhones cannot from a web page, so they chime instead.)
- Keep it all on theme, with the branding.
- Unless otherwise cued, every active screen shows a looping graphic. (Jon's line was cut off after "every active screen should have a"; read as the loops the cues describe. Jon corrects it if that is wrong.)
- Build note (25 Oct, evening, step 3): cue 5's video column says the Parlour returns to the loop right after the blackout. As built, every screen stays dark through the five seconds and the role cards, until the first Next of Act 2, so every eye is on its own phone; the lights and the music come back as cue 5 says. The question promptScreen on the board lets Jon choose the loop instead. The role card has no early-continue tap: its fifteen seconds always run, and the role stays on the You tab.
