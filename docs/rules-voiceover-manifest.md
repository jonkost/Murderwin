Written 10 November 2026. Jon records the narrator for the 28 rules slides, then hands the audio to Claude Code, which lines each slide up with its audio and adds a bumper around it.

Source of truth for the words: the **Intro and Rules Script** doc (Parts 2 and 3). The same lines are in each slide's speaker notes in the rules deck, under `FILE:`.

## How to record

- **One audio file per slide**, named exactly as the File column below (for example `rules-07-your-character.wav`). The name is what ties the audio to the slide.
- WAV or M4A, 48 kHz, mono is fine. Leave about half a second of quiet at the start and end of each take; Code trims it.
- A retake just replaces the file with the same name.

## What Code should build

1. Export the deck as a PDF (Slides download), and turn each page into a 1920×1080 image. Page N = slide N below.
2. For each slide: **bumper in** (0.75 s of the slide, silent) → the audio → **bumper out** (1.0 s, silent). Cross-fade 0.3 s between slides. Adjust to taste.
3. Missing audio for a slide? Stop and list it rather than guessing.
4. Soft gramophone jazz bed under the whole thing, ducked under the voice (see the script doc's "The voice and the look").
5. Output one video: `rules-video.mp4`, 1920×1080. Also write a cue sheet (slide, start time, end time) so the cue list can point at it.

Nothing here is sealed. No case content goes in this video.

## Full lines

**rules-01-cover**

> Every house has its rules. This one has rather more than most. Listen closely. You will only hear them once. Well… twice. They are also on your phone.

**rules-02-tonight**

> Tonight, someone at this party is murdered. By the end of the evening, you must name three things. Who did it. Why they did it. And who, exactly, died. Name all three… and you win.

**rules-03-three-roles**

> Most of you are Detectives. You know nothing yet. Splendid. One of you will be the Murderer, who knows they did it, but cannot for the life of them remember why. And one of you will be the Ghost, who knows they are dead, but not who is responsible. The roles arrive in the dark, at the end of act one. When the power comes back, every phone shows the same button. Tap it, read your card, and keep a straight face. Nobody stands out.

**rules-04-the-ghost**

> Now, the ghost rule. One of us dies tonight. We can all still see them. None of us has noticed. If you think you have spotted the Ghost, say so. Nothing happens. And if you are the Ghost… you never confirm it. Not until the very end. You still need to work out who did it, and why. And at the vote, name yourself.

**rules-05-the-murderer**

> And if you are the Murderer… you did it, and you cannot for the life of you remember why. Keep your secret. Never confess. Lie, deflect, point fingers. All allowed. But you still need the motive and the victim, like everyone else. Hide too well… and you blind yourself. And at the vote, yes. Name yourself.

**rules-06-everyone-wins**

> There are no teams. Everyone wins the same way. Murderer, plus Motive, plus Victim. Even the Murderer. Even the Ghost. Each of them holds just one piece. At the end, most of the house decides whether the Murderer is caught. But your answer is your own.

**rules-07-your-character**

> When you arrive, scan the code on the Parlour TV, or the card by the door, and type your name. Then tap Allow, so your phone can chime, feel a shake, and stay awake. The Manor will hand you a character. A stranger's name, a stranger's trade. It is yours all night. No swapping. Your bag number is on your card. Go and collect it. Tell everyone who you are and what you do. The rest… you may keep to yourself.

**rules-08-your-phone**

> Your phone is your notebook, your map and your memory. Use your own, and do not share it. Tap Allow when it asks, and turn off auto-lock if you can. If it dies, sign in on a spare, and your character follows you. And if a red strip appears, the Manor simply cannot hear you for a moment. Stay put. Nothing is lost.

**rules-09-your-reticule**

> Everything you carry lives in your Reticule: a small drawstring bag, on your phone. Seven tabs. Now: what to do this minute. You: your character. Clues: everything you've been told. Notes: yours alone. Motives: tap one to cross it off. Cats: how each one feels about you. And Rules: all of this, all night.

**rules-10-motives**

> There are nineteen possible motives. Each one is a little rhyme, and every one is on your phone from the start. Exactly one is true tonight. Cross off the rest as you go. Nobody will do it for you. And be warned. They are petty. Very petty. Anyone could have done it… for any of them.

**rules-11-the-household**

> Meet the household. Witnesses, never suspects. Sir Irwin, the birthday boy, in the Parlour. He never lies, though he is only one, and knows less than he thinks. Granolia Lickspittle, in the Study. Honest, and forgetful. Miss Boo LaRue, in the Entryway, who says more the more she likes you. Bore her, and you get a look. Zimothy Clawford, Esquire, the old family solicitor, in the Library. He never lies… but if he doesn't like you, you get the cheap version. And Salem Crooknog, the groundskeeper, in the Orangerie. Wrong more often than not. Never once in doubt.

**rules-12-irwin-is-right**

> If you remember only one thing tonight, remember this. If Irwin and anyone disagree… Irwin is right.

**rules-13-how-cats-feel**

> Every cat has an opinion of you. The more they like you, the more they tell you. Fond of you? The whole story. Warming up? Some of it. Not keen? Very little. No harm done. Your Cats tab shows where you stand with each one, from cold shoulder… all the way to devoted. In words, never numbers. And Sir Irwin is locked at devoted. He adores everyone.

**rules-14-reading-the-household**

> A tip. How much a cat tells you depends on how it feels about you. How true it is depends on the cat. Sir Irwin never lies. Use him to check everyone else. Granolia never lies either, but the less she likes you, the more she forgets. Miss Boo says more the more she likes you. Zimothy never lies, but if he doesn't like you, you get the cheap version. And Salem, most days, has it backwards. So hear him out… then flip it.

**rules-15-parlour-games**

> Twelve times tonight, Miss Boo LaRue hosts a Parlour Game. Five in act two. Five in act four. Two to open act six. In acts two and six, every phone lights up at the same moment, and you have one minute. In act four, your games wait on your phone until you're free. Three silly questions. Tap your answers. There are no wrong ones. Only opinions. And the cats will form theirs accordingly. Your business with the cats… is yours.

**rules-16-your-bag**

> When you sign in, your phone gives you a bag number. Go pick it up. Inside are four or five small things. Peek in your own whenever you like, but keep it closed unless you invite someone in. Want to sniff, feel or shake someone else's? Ask first. And listen. Some things rattle. Now, the big one. In act two, every bag clue is about the Murderer. In act four, every bag clue is about the Ghost. The bags and the traces are the only things tonight that ever point at a person.

**rules-17-traces**

> Everyone leaves something behind. Every character carries a trace, right there on their portrait. Twice tonight, a trace turns up. In act four, Granolia's game in the Study shows the Ghost's trace. In act six, when the bedroom door opens, you will see the Murderer's. It may be hazy. It may come with a look-alike. Match it to a portrait… and you have a name.

**rules-18-house-rules**

> Four house rules. When you hear the chime, look at your phone. Something is about to happen. Nothing travels phone to phone, so share with your mouth. Argue. Fib if you must. Keep your eyes on your own phone. Some screens move on by themselves. That's normal. And if it ever says Sir Irwin requires the garden… the game is paused. Carry on.

**rules-19-the-night**

> The night comes in seven acts. One sunset. And nobody leaves… before the truth. The times are rough. The food decides. Your phone always tells you which act we're in.

**rules-20-act1-the-invitation**

> Act one. The Invitation. You are in it now. Drinks are poured. Characters are dealt. Nobody has a secret… yet. Enjoy it. And keep an eye on the window. The sun sets at five thirty-two.

**rules-21-act2-lights-out**

> Act two. Lights Out. At sunset, the power fails. When it returns, a life has been taken, and every phone in the house lights up. Then W-O-O-F News comes on the TV. Take a quiet look in your own bag. Your phone sends you to all four cats, one at a time, alone. Granolia in the Study. Zimothy in the Library. Salem in the Orangerie. Boo in the Entryway. Every cat gives you a motive hint, and a clue about the Murderer's bag. Not ready when it calls you? Tap Not yet, call me later. No penalty. Between visits, wait in the Parlour. Sir Irwin has ideas on the TV. And whatever the cats tell you… is for your ears only.

**rules-22-act3-supper-is-served**

> Act three. Supper Is Served. The family insists dinner goes ahead. Phones stay charging in the Parlour. Bring your bag to the table. The radio crackles. Somewhere in the static, W-O-O-F News. When the news comes on, hush. Everyone hears it at once. Missed it? The words stay up on the iPad. Argue about it over dinner. And after supper, cross off the motives you've ruled out.

**rules-23-act4-behind-closed-doors**

> Act four. Behind Closed Doors. The house splits into small groups. Your phone puts you in a pair or a trio. Tap Locked in. Your group plays four rooms, fifteen minutes each. Upstairs in the Study, Granolia's Lightbulb Moment. In the Library, Zimothy's In Duplicate. In the Orangerie, Salem's Get Off My Lawn. Backwards, of course. And in the Entryway, Miss Boo LaRue's Arcade. Five games each, and the top scores are remembered. When the chime sounds, every group moves on. Finished or not, every room gives you something. Any bag clue now is about the Ghost. Between rooms, wait in the Parlour with Sir Irwin, and play the Parlour Games waiting on your phone. And what you learn stays inside your group.

**rules-24-act5-one-candle**

> Act five. One Candle. One candle, one year, one very good boy. Sir Irwin's birthday cake. We sing. Wander where you like. And when the chime calls you to the Parlour, watch the big screen. The birthday boy has something to say about the motive. And remember… he never lies.

**rules-25-act6-the-final-hour**

> Act six. The Final Hour. Everyone in one room. Every suspicion out loud. Nowhere left to hide. Two last Parlour Games. Then, Sir Irwin Needs a Hand. Three drawings open the door… and he only has paws. The Manor calls one of us to the paper in the Library. The rest of us describe the square on the screen. Words only. Fifteen seconds a turn. Name all three pictures, and the bedroom door opens, with the Murderer's trace inside. Then each of us says, out loud, one thing we have ruled out. Your last chance… to change your mind.

**rules-26-act7-unmasked**

> Act seven. Unmasked. Salem gives you two minutes. Three names. Pick your Murderer. Your Motive. Your Victim. Your clues and notes stay open, so use them. Then Sir Irwin tallies up the night: the stats, the awards, and the Arcade's top scores. And then W-O-O-F News breaks the story, and the masks come off. The Murderer first. Then the Motive. And last of all… who it was that died. Then the credits roll.

**rules-27-keep-your-secret**

> One last thing. If the Murderer or the Ghost gives themselves away, the Manor may shuffle the case. New evidence will come to light. So if you find yourself holding a secret tonight… keep it.

**rules-28-good-luck**

> That is everything. Good luck. Trust the dog. And do keep an eye on the clock. The sun… is going down.
