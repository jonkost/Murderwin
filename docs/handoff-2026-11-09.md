## 1 · Decided by Jon today

**The cue 20 screen (Tallying the submissions, the Parlour TV)**

- It is one 16:9 screen.
- Irwin's progress bar sits in the top third of the screen. (Jon first said the centre, then moved it up the same evening.)
- The achievements and stats come in and out underneath the bar, one at a time. They get the bottom two thirds of the screen, so there is room for long ones.
- The bar loads for two minutes.
- When the bar is full, the TV cuts to the newscast (cue 21).

**Irwin's animation**

- The art is Jon's own: Irwin as one flat colour, with a butterfly.
- Irwin runs along the bar at a steady, bouncy pace, chasing the butterfly.
- In the middle he stops for a play bow ("downward facing dog"): elbows down, rump up, tail wagging. The butterfly hovers over his nose. Then he runs to catch up.
- At 100% the butterfly flies off and Irwin stops to watch it go.
- Irwin is small: about 8% of the bar's length.
- He keeps his nose pointed at the butterfly the whole time, and the butterfly stays just in front of his nose.
- It is made in code. There is no After Effects file, no Lottie file and no plugin.

## 2 · The file

`docs/irwin-progress-bar.html` (copied in 14 Nov). Preview: https://claude.ai/artifact/DjMJwZekYDcYdzQVKbgAtC

- One file. No outside code, no images, no build step. About 26 KB.
- It loads one web font (Source Serif 4, from Google Fonts) and falls back to Georgia.
- The art is traced from Jon's picture into vector shapes, so it stays sharp at any size.
- Colours: Irwin `#CD6300`, butterfly `#6BE4CF`, background `#001925`, text `#F1E8D6`.
- Opened plainly, it is a preview page with sample achievements and a Play button. The samples are marked "Sample" and are not real results.
- Opened with `?embed=1`, it shows only the 16:9 screen, filling the window, and waits to be started.

## 3 · How to wire it

| To do this | Send this |
| --- | --- |
| Start the screen | `{type:'irwin:start', seconds:120, elapsed:0, cards:[...]}` posted to the frame, or `IrwinBar.start({...})` |
| Rejoin after a reload | The same message, with `elapsed` set to the seconds already gone on the server's clock |
| Know it has finished | The frame posts `{type:'irwin:done'}` to its parent, once, about 1.6 seconds after the bar is full. `IrwinBar.start` also takes `onDone`. |
| Drive the bar by hand instead | `{type:'irwin:progress', p:0 to 1}` or `IrwinBar.setProgress(p)` |
| Change the cards | `{type:'irwin:cards', cards:[...]}` or `IrwinBar.setCards([...])` |

A card is `{tag, title, detail}`, all plain text.

**The cards share the time evenly.** They show between 3% and 97% of the bar. Keep each up at least 5 seconds.

**Settings near the top of the script:** `S` (Irwin's size), `BOW` (when the bow starts and ends, as progress), `CARD_SPAN`, `DONE_AFTER`.

## 4 · Rules that still hold

- **Rule 1.** The real cards come from the server's finale doc. Never in the repo, a fixture or Jon's terminal. Tests use stand-in cards.
- **No card may hint at a role.** They show before the reveal.
- The wording of each card is Jon's. Do not invent new award names.
