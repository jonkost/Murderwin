# Read aloud: a script for Jon

Jon, 11 Nov 2026: "If there are things that need to be read out loud, I just
need a script for it." On 12 Nov the phones and the room screens lost every
line a guest does not need to play. The words below are the ones worth saying
to the room instead. Say them in your own way. Nothing here is a secret.

Each heading is a beat, in the night's order. Skip any you like: the game
works without them.

## Act 1 · Meet the household

"Four cats and the birthday boy live here. Your Cats tab shows how each one
feels about you."

## Act 1 · Introductions

"Say your name and your profession out loud. Nothing else."

## Act 2 · Orientation

"The tabs at the bottom of your phone: Clues keeps what you are told, Notes is
yours alone, and Motives is where you cross things off."

## Act 2 · The cat visits

"When it is your turn, go alone. What a cat tells you is for you only. Tap
Done when you have read it."

## Act 2 · Supper

"Once you have seen all four cats, tell nobody what you heard. The chime calls
everyone to supper."

## Act 3 · Phones down

"Leave your phone charging in the Parlour and bring your bag to the Orangerie.
At the table you may share what is in it."

## Act 4 · Two-minute warning

"Behind Closed Doors starts in two minutes. Keep your phone close."

## Act 4 · Waiting in the Parlour

A group with no room this round waits in the Parlour. If they look stuck, any
of these: "Have you crossed off everything you know?" "Compare what the cats
told you." "Remember, Salem usually has it backwards." "Look at the portraits:
every character carries a trace." "Only tell your group."

## Act 5 · Cake

"Go where you like. You may talk about the bags now, out loud, in any detail."

## Act 6 · Two Parlour Games

"Two Parlour Games, then the final event in the Parlour. Stretch your legs."

## Act 6 · The door opens

"The hall light is green. Go and see what the bedroom shows, then come back to
the Parlour."
